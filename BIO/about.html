Aiden Voss

"I design systems that shape how humans and machines connect."

I'm Aiden Voss, a human-machine interaction designer based in Zurich. I lead interaction design for XR and embodied systems at TechCorp, where I work at the intersection of neural interfaces, extended reality, and accessible design.

Before TechCorp, I spent four years designing prosthetic user experiences at a medtech startup, and two years before that building mixed reality prototypes at a research lab in Berlin. I've also worked in film VFX, which is where I first became obsessed with the idea that perception can be designed.

On the side I lecture at ETH Zürich, ZHdK, and the Royal College of Art in London, write on Medium, and mentor designers who are trying to break into the XR and embodiment space.
